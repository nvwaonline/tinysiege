生效日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日
开发者：Rulith
联系邮箱：michaltina@hotmail.com

一、概述

方寸战争（以下简称"本游戏"）是一款在 PICO 头显上运行的单机混合现实塔防游戏。我们非常重视你的隐私。本游戏不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人信息。

二、我们处理哪些信息

本游戏只在你的头显本地保存以下游戏数据，用于记住你的进度：

数据内容保存位置
游戏进度已解锁的关卡、每关星级、无尽模式最高纪录、选择的难度头显本地（应用私有存储）
试玩记录每局的开始时间、关卡、难度、结果、坚持的波数、建造和升级的塔、选择的卡牌等游戏内数据头显本地（应用私有存储）

这些数据不含姓名、账号、设备标识、位置、声音、图像等个人信息，不会发送给开发者或任何第三方。卸载本游戏后，这些数据会被一并删除。

本游戏使用头显的透视（passthrough）画面和手柄位置来显示和操作游戏，这些画面和位置数据只在头显上实时使用，不会被保存或上传。

三、权限说明

权限用途
网络访问（android.permission.INTERNET）由游戏引擎和 PICO SDK 声明，供 PICO 平台完成正版验证等功能。游戏本身不通过网络收发其他数据。
修改系统设置（android.permission.WRITE_SETTINGS）由 PICO SDK 声明。游戏本身不会修改你的系统设置。

四、购买和正版验证

本游戏在 PICO 商店付费下载，买一次就能玩全部内容，游戏里没有任何购买。付款由 PICO 平台处理，我们收不到你的支付信息、账号或身份信息。游戏启动时会通过 PICO 平台确认当前账号已经购买本游戏（正版验证），验证结果只用来决定游戏能否运行，不会被保存，也不会发送给我们。

六、未成年人保护

本游戏不收集任何个人信息，包括未成年人的个人信息。未成年人请在监护人指导下使用 VR 设备，并注意适度游戏、合理休息。

Tiny Siege Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026
Developer: Rulith
Contact: michaltina@hotmail.com

Tiny Siege is a single-player mixed-reality tower defense game for PICO headsets. The game does not collect, upload or sell any personal information.

Data stored on your headset only. The game saves your progress (unlocked levels, stars, endless records, chosen difficulty) and a local play log (time, level, difficulty, result, waves reached, towers built, cards picked) in the app's private storage on your headset. This data contains no personal information, is never sent to the developer or any third party, and is deleted when you uninstall the game. Passthrough video and controller positions are used in real time on the headset only and are never stored or uploaded.

Permissions. INTERNET is declared by the game engine and the PICO SDK for platform features such as license verification; the game itself sends and receives no other data. WRITE_SETTINGS is declared by the PICO SDK; the game does not change your system settings.

Purchase and license check. The game is a paid download from the PICO Store: one purchase unlocks everything, and there are no purchases inside the game. PICO handles the payment; we never receive your payment details, account or identity. At start the game asks PICO whether the current account owns it (license verification); the answer is only used to decide whether the game may run and is neither stored nor sent to us.

Third-party SDKs. PICO XR SDK (display, controllers, passthrough, license verification and other platform features; governed by PICO's privacy policy). Unity engine (Unity Analytics, crash reporting and performance reporting are turned off; the engine may send anonymous device hardware statistics to Unity at startup).

Children. The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
